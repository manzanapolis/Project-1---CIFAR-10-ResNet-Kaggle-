# Design decisions

Statistics referenced here come from `exploration/data_diagnostics.py`.

Format: rows are self-contained (no cross-references between rows). When a decision is to be A/B tested, it gets sub-rows (e.g. 3A, 3B), one per variant; A is the default. Data-layer rows are grouped ETL-style: Extract (E), Transform (T), Load (L), Inference (I); each list numbers its own rows. Source: ✓ CIFAR-10 = the paper reports results on CIFAR-10; every method in this table must be cited in the report and repo.

### Transform: augmentation and normalization

Execution order: geometric augmentation → photometric augmentation → normalization → Cutout (when enabled); CutMix (default) runs in the training loop.

### Load: splits and serving to the model

| # | Problem | Considered, rejected | Solution | ML method | Implementation | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | **Model selection without test leakage**: every design choice needs an accuracy estimate, and the hidden test set may not be used for selection (the public leaderboard is also noisy: 1,000 images, SE ≈ 0.7–0.9 pt, and fitting it hurts the private half that decides rankings). Expected gaps between variants (~0.1–0.4 pt) are at or below the SE of a 5k-image holdout (≈0.31 pt at 95%). Copies of the same photo (2.2% of training images) must not straddle a split, or validation rewards memorization. Validation data comes from the training distribution, while the test set is shifted (KS 0.06–0.07; 3× more grayscale). | **Single 5k holdout**: SE ≈ 0.31 pt, can't resolve most expected gaps, and one model per variant gives no estimate of run-to-run spread. **10-fold CV**: same precision as 5-fold (every image is validated once either way) at twice the runs and wall-clock; its advantage (fold models on 45k, closer to the final fit) matters for distillation teachers, not for ranking variants. **Image-level (ungrouped) folds**: split duplicate groups across train/validation. **Unstratified folds**: add class-mix noise. **Tuning on the public leaderboard**: against the rules outside the official process, noisy, overfits the public half. **Selecting the best epoch on held-out data per run**: optimistic; fixed-length training is evaluated at its last epoch. **Augmented validation images**: measures a different distribution from test. **Comparing accuracies independently**: weaker than paired testing on the same images. | Every variant is trained 5 times, once per fold (train on 40k, validate on the held-out 10k), giving one out-of-fold prediction for each of the 50,000 training images. Compare variants **paired** on the 50k out-of-fold predictions (McNemar; resolves ~0.1–0.15 pt gaps) and by per-fold differences; when two variants are within one standard error, choose the simpler one.
