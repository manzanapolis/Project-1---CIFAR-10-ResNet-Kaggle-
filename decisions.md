# Design decisions

Statistics referenced here come from `exploration/data_diagnostics.py`.

Format: rows are self-contained (no cross-references between rows). When a decision is to be A/B tested, it gets sub-rows (e.g. 3A, 3B), one per variant; A is the default. Data-layer rows are grouped ETL-style: Extract (E), Transform (T), Load (L), Inference (I); each list numbers its own rows. Source: ✓ CIFAR-10 = the paper reports results on CIFAR-10; every method in this table must be cited in the report and repo.

### Transform: augmentation and normalization

Execution order: geometric augmentation → photometric augmentation (or a rendition, for the images picked for one) → normalization → Cutout (when enabled); CutMix (default) runs in the training loop.

### Load: splits and serving to the model

| # | Problem | Considered, rejected | Solution | ML method | Implementation | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | **Model selection without test leakage**: every design choice needs an accuracy estimate, and the hidden test set may not be used for selection (the public leaderboard is also noisy: 1,000 images, SE ≈ 0.7–0.9 pt, and fitting it hurts the private half that decides rankings). Expected gaps between variants (~0.1–0.4 pt) are at or below the SE of a 5k-image holdout (≈0.31 pt at 95%). Copies of the same photo (2.2% of training images) must not straddle a split, or validation rewards memorization.
